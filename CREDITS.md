# Credits

## Body map artwork

The front and back anatomy figures behind the muscle heat map (`app/src/main/assets/body_map.txt`) are by **Ryan Graves**.

- Original: [Figma Community](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1320468164820924031), as packaged in the "advanced" style of [muscle_mapper](https://github.com/suryamolly/muscle_mapper)
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes made for Tonnage: converted from SVG to a plain text list of shapes, coordinates rounded, recoloured with shading, each muscle tagged with one of the exercise library's muscle groups and a name, and lower-back (spinal erector) shapes added. The conversion script is `tools/body-map/convert.py`.

The app shows this credit under More > Credits.

## Exercise library

`app/src/main/assets/exercises.json` is a trimmed copy of [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released into the public domain.
